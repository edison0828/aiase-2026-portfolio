Research workflow for this topic:

1. Define a target workload before selecting a storage optimization.
2. State which metric matters most: throughput, average latency, P99 latency, endurance, or recovery cost.
3. Identify which layer owns each complexity: application, file system, runtime, device firmware, or media.
4. Separate foreground request behavior from background work such as garbage collection or compaction.
5. Evaluate under realistic fill levels and enough runtime to expose steady-state behavior.
6. Compare not only wins but also regressions under mismatched workloads.
7. Record open questions for future paper collection, especially zoned storage, NVM consistency, and AI data pipeline integration.
