System Research: designing computing infrastructure under real-world constraints.
Design Space: the set of feasible architectural choices and their trade-offs.
Tail Latency: the high-percentile latency such as P99 or P99.9.
FTL: Flash Translation Layer inside an SSD.
Garbage Collection: background cleanup that reclaims invalid data.
Write Amplification: physical writes divided by logical writes.
SMR: Shingled Magnetic Recording.
IMR: Interlaced Magnetic Recording or related dense magnetic layout strategies.
NVM: Non-Volatile Memory.
Crash Consistency: preserving correctness after failures or power loss.
LSM-tree: log-structured merge tree used by many KV stores.
Compaction: background merge and reorganization work in LSM systems.
Device-aware: software that explicitly adapts to media or device rules.
Co-Design: hardware and software designed together rather than independently.
